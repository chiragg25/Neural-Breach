HOW TO PLAY | MGM SYNERGY 2026

1. SETTING & FACTIONS

Aetheria Deep-Space Bio-Neural Facility (Sector-07). At match start, players are secretly assigned roles:

  • RESEARCH OPERATIVES: Complete 8 interactive calibration tasks around the station and vote out the hidden impostors.
  • INFILTRATORS: Secretly eliminate operatives in isolated corridors and trigger sabotages.

2. INTERACTIVE TASKS

Approach consoles (yellow beacons) and press E or tap USE:

  • Circuit Matrix: Connect matching colored cables across terminals.
  • Quantum Core: Adjust harmonic slider to match station resonance wave.
  • Security Cypher: Enter 5-digit encrypted passcode from badge.
  • Bio-Sample Centrifuge: Detect & sterilize the mutated vial.
  • Memory Reboot: Simon-says style pattern reproduction.
  • Array Alignment: Turn antenna dial to lock 100% signal.

3. SABOTAGE & EMERGENCIES

Infiltrators can trigger station emergencies. Reactor Meltdown gives operatives 35 seconds to reach both terminals (A & B) and press overrides, or Infiltrators win!

4. MEETINGS & TRIBUNALS

Report fallen bodies or call an Emergency Tribunal from the Central Nexus. Living players debate in chat and vote on who to eject into deep space.
